# About Quantum Mechanics: Approximation Methods and Real Systems

GitHub repository: [quantum-mechanics-vol3](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/quantum-mechanics-vol3)

Author: Nik Bear Brown

## Book Summary

`quantum-mechanics-vol3` is organized as a chapter-based book project. The chapters below outline the book's main instructional arc and source files.

## Chapter Summary

1. **Chapter 1 — Time-Independent Perturbation Theory** (`chapters/01-time-independent-perturbation-theory.md`) - Most real quantum systems cannot be solved exactly.
2. **Chapter 2 — Degenerate Perturbation Theory and Fine Structure** (`chapters/02-degenerate-perturbation-theory-and-fine-structure.md`) - In 1913, Johannes Stark applied an electric field of about $10^5$ V/cm to hydrogen gas and observed the Balmer lines split.
3. **Chapter 3 — The Variational Principle** (`chapters/03-the-variational-principle.md`) - In 1929, Egil Hylleraas was working in Copenhagen on the ground-state energy of helium.
4. **Chapter 4 — The WKB Approximation and Tunneling** (`chapters/04-the-wkb-approximation-and-tunneling.md`) - Polonium-212 has a half-life of about $3\times10^{-7}$ seconds.
5. **Chapter 5 — Time-Dependent Perturbation Theory and Transitions** (`chapters/05-time-dependent-perturbation-theory-and-transitions.md`) - In January 1938 at Columbia University, Isidor Rabi's group passed a beam of LiCl molecules through three magnets.
6. **Chapter 6 — Radiation and Fermi's Golden Rule** (`chapters/06-radiation-and-fermis-golden-rule.md`) - On October 4, 1947, Lamb and Retherford published their result in *Physical Review*: the $2s_{1/2}$ and $2p_{1/2}$ levels of hydrogen, predicted by Dirac's equation to be degenerate, are separated by about 1,060 MHz.
7. **Chapter 7 — Scattering I: Partial Waves** (`chapters/07-scattering-i-partial-waves.md`) - Consider a hard sphere of radius $a$ placed in the laboratory.
8. **Chapter 8 — Scattering II: The Born Approximation** (`chapters/08-scattering-ii-the-born-approximation.md`) - In 1911, Ernest Rutherford calculated the scattering of alpha particles off gold nuclei using classical mechanics.
9. **Chapter 9 — Atoms in Fields: Zeeman, Stark, and Magnetic Resonance** (`chapters/09-atoms-in-fields.md`) - In 1896, Pieter Zeeman investigated whether a strong magnetic field would change the color of spectral lines emitted by a flame.
10. **Chapter 10 — Periodic Potentials and the Band Structure of Solids** (`chapters/10-periodic-potentials-and-band-structure.md`) - A central result of solid-state physics is that a perfectly periodic potential does not scatter electrons.
11. **Chapter 11 — Capstone: Modeling a Real Quantum System** (`chapters/11-capstone-modeling-a-real-quantum-system.md`) - Gordon Moore, working at Fairchild Semiconductor in 1965, observed that transistor density was doubling roughly every two years.
